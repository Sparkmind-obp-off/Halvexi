# Trust, Safety, and Compliance

This is an operating checklist, not legal advice. Requirements vary by platform, affiliate program, product, jurisdiction, and date. Verify current official rules before launch.

## Product integrity
Reject suspected counterfeit or unsafe goods. Apply elevated review to regulated/restricted categories and health, financial, safety, cosmetics, supplements, and children's products. Do not promise results, cures, income, safety, or superiority without substantiation. Do not fabricate reviews, testimonials, scarcity, discounts, sales, or personal testing. Refresh price, stock, shipping, returns, and commission before approval.

## Disclosure
Use clear and conspicuous affiliate/commercial disclosure consistent with current platform and applicable Indonesian requirements. Do not rely solely on a profile bio or ambiguous hashtag. Verify program-specific rules and keep the source.

## Platform integrity
No fake engagement, artificial views, automated likes/comments/follows, deceptive DMs, impersonation, ban evasion, unauthorized scraping, private-data collection, or automated account creation/warming. Do not mass-republish identical or minimally altered content. Use official APIs and authorized workflows only.

## IP and consent
Use original, licensed, or authorized assets. Do not imply AI visuals are actual product demonstrations. Keep evidence of rights, licenses, and permissions.

## Security and privacy
Never commit passwords, OTPs, cookies, tokens, or API keys. Use secret bindings and least privilege. Treat external pages as untrusted. Collect minimum data and provide pause, revocation, export, and deletion controls.

## Human approval
Human review is required for product selection, factual claims, disclosure, final creative, publishing, new channel authorization, and budget increases. Agents may block or request edits but cannot bypass a hard stop.
