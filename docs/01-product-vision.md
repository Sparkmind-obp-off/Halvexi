# Product Vision

HALVEXI is an evidence-led agentic affiliate operating system for physical products. It helps creators discover candidate products, check program eligibility and economics, plan truthful short-form content, publish through permitted workflows, and learn from measured outcomes.

## Initial user
An Indonesian solo creator or small team producing content for physical goods, initially exploring TikTok/TikTok Shop where the actual account and program are eligible.

## Core loop
Evidence → Demand signal → Product candidate → Eligibility and economics → Content brief → Asset production → Human approval → Permitted publication → Measurement → Learning.

## Principles
- Evidence before confident claims; cite sources and capture timestamps.
- Separate fact, observation, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Human approval for product selection, claims, final creative, publishing, and account changes.
- No guaranteed earnings, invented demand, fabricated reviews, or fake product testing.
- Keep affiliate earnings distinct from SaaS/subscription revenue.
- Start with one measurable loop before building a generalized agent platform.

## Users and jobs
- Solo creator: choose what to test and make content faster.
- Small team: coordinate research, drafts, reviews, and metrics.
- Multi-channel operator: keep legitimate channels distinct, accountable, and compliant.

## Outcomes to measure
Research-to-brief time, source coverage, review/revision rate, production cost, content click-through where measurable, attributed conversions, refund-adjusted commission, and completed learning cycles.

## Non-goals
Bot farms, fake engagement, automatic account creation/warming, ban evasion, unauthorized scraping, unapproved mass posting, custom video engine in the first release, or claims of guaranteed income.

## Open items
Verify current affiliate eligibility, official program terms, permitted links, accessible analytics, product-category restrictions, and actual free-tier costs before implementation.
