# Content Production System

## Per-concept deliverables
Content ID, product ID, channel, audience, hypothesis, evidence-backed angle, three hook options, timed script, shot list, on-screen text, caption, affiliate disclosure, CTA, claim ledger, required assets, rights status, reviewer, version, approval, and measurement plan.

## Physical-product formats
- Demonstration of a real, observable function.
- Problem/method/result using only results actually observed.
- Feature walkthrough with verified specifications and limitations.
- Fair comparison of like-for-like attributes.
- Setup/how-to based on instructions.
- Use-case scenario clearly labelled when reenacted.
- Buyer checklist and FAQ based on sourced facts.

## Reusable brief
Audience/problem; evidence; hypothesis; hook; script; shots; overlay text; caption; disclosure; CTA; claims and sources; assets to capture; risk checks; success metric; reviewer/status.

## Production stages
Research → concept → brief → footage capture or rights-cleared asset selection → edit → claim review → disclosure check → human approval → permitted publication → measurement.

## Quality rubric
Score 0–2 each: accuracy/provenance, hook relevance, demonstration clarity, audience fit, readability, differentiation, disclosure/CTA, asset feasibility, policy fit, testability. Suggested initial threshold: 16/20 and no hard-stop issue. This threshold is a hypothesis, not a validated predictor.

## Asset integrity
Do not reuse other creators' videos, photos, music, or reviews without permission/license. AI visuals must not be presented as actual product footage. If the creator has not tested a product, use an honest researched overview and never imply first-hand experience.
