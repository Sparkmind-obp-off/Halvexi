# Launch Readiness Checklist

## Account and program
- [ ] The operator controls the account and has reviewed current official account requirements.
- [ ] Affiliate program approval/eligibility is verified in the actual account.
- [ ] The exact permitted link method is known.
- [ ] The current platform/program disclosure rules are recorded with official source and date.
- [ ] The product category is allowed for this account/program/region.

## Product and content
- [ ] Product source, price, and key facts are current.
- [ ] Commission terms are confirmed, not guessed.
- [ ] All claims are supported by the evidence ledger.
- [ ] The creator's experience is represented truthfully.
- [ ] Original or rights-cleared footage/audio is available.
- [ ] Disclosure is clear in the content/caption as required.
- [ ] Product link resolves to the intended destination.
- [ ] A human approved the exact version.

## Measurement
- [ ] Content ID, channel, product, version, publication time are recorded.
- [ ] Tracking method and attribution limitations are documented.
- [ ] Primary metric, guardrails, and observation window are set.
- [ ] The team knows which data is observable and which is unavailable.

## Technical readiness before deployment
- [ ] Authentication and authorization are tested.
- [ ] Secrets are stored in provider secret storage, not source control.
- [ ] Database migrations and export/backup are tested.
- [ ] Rate limits, budget limits, audit events, and pause switch are tested.
- [ ] External content is sanitized and treated as untrusted.
- [ ] No automatic posting or engagement is enabled without a verified official integration and explicit approval.

## Go/no-go rule
Any missing eligibility, disclosure, rights, or unsupported-claim requirement means **no-go** for that product/content item. Technical deployment does not equal platform approval or legal clearance.
