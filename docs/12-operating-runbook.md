# Operating Runbook

## Daily workflow
1. Review candidate evidence and freshness.
2. Select a small number for eligibility/economics review.
3. Reject hard-stop products.
4. Select a product with a truthful visual demonstration.
5. Draft three meaningfully different concepts.
6. Review sources, assets/rights, disclosure, CTA, destination link, and policy checklist.
7. Human approves the exact version.
8. Publish manually or through a separately verified authorized integration.
9. Record post URL/ID, channel, product, version, timestamp, and tracking method.
10. Import metrics at a defined interval and note gaps.
11. Record learning and next hypothesis.

## Pre-publish checklist
- [ ] Product/program/account eligibility verified in current official source.
- [ ] Price, stock, shipping, returns, and commission claims current or omitted.
- [ ] Factual claims have sources.
- [ ] No false personal experience, fabricated testimonial, or false urgency.
- [ ] Footage/music/assets rights-cleared.
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure clear.
- [ ] Product link resolves.
- [ ] Channel authorized and in good standing.
- [ ] Human approval matches exact content version.
- [ ] Measurement plan exists.

## Incident response
Unsupported claim: pause related drafts, correct/remove claim, review published content, log remediation.
Ineligible product/program: stop queued work, verify official terms, update candidate, review live content/links.
Account warning: pause activity on affected channel; do not create replacement accounts to evade enforcement; use legitimate appeal/review routes.
Cost runaway: pause queue, disable adapter, inspect retries and logs, reset limits only after review.
Credential exposure: revoke/rotate through provider, inspect audit trail, never paste credentials into repository or chat.

## Weekly review
Review strongest evidence, production time, revisions, metrics by compatible definitions, confirmed commission/refunds, compliance incidents, stale records, and next test hypothesis.
