# Measurement and Experiments

## Learning north star
Compliant, source-backed content experiments completed with usable outcome data per week. This measures learning throughput, not earnings by itself.

## Funnel
Research → accepted candidate → brief → approved content → publication → reach/views → clicks → attributed conversions → confirmed/adjusted commission → payout.

## Metrics
Production time/cost/revisions; views/reach/watch time if available; clicks and CTR only with compatible definitions; attributed conversions; gross and refund-adjusted commission; payout status; claim correction rate; disclosure errors; source freshness; policy flags.

## Definitions
CTR = clicks / impressions only when the platform provides compatible metrics. Conversion rate = attributed conversions / clicks only when attribution definitions align. Net commission deducts refunds and attributable costs only when known. Do not combine platform metrics with different definitions without caveats.

## Experiment record
Hypothesis, product, audience, channel, content version, primary metric, guardrails, start/end time, observation window, concurrent changes, actual sample, data gaps, result (supported/rejected/inconclusive), next action.

Change one major creative variable at a time when practical. Organic distribution is noisy; small samples are directional, not proof of causality. High views without clicks are not automatically a win. One sale is not proof of repeatable demand. Preserve failed and inconclusive tests.
