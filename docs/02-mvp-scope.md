# MVP Scope and Release Gates

## MVP objective
Prove one complete, compliant physical-product affiliate content loop before expanding automation.

## Included
1. Manual product intake and source links.
2. Evidence ledger with source, excerpt, timestamp, confidence, freshness.
3. Eligibility and economics checklist.
4. Explainable product score with missing-data flags and hard stops.
5. Content brief, scripts, shot list, caption, disclosure, CTA, and claim ledger.
6. Versioned human review and approval.
7. Manual publishing checklist and publication record.
8. Manual metrics entry/import, experiment notes, and export.
9. Agent run, audit, and cost logs.

## Excluded
Automated account creation, engagement, DMs, comments, follows, fake views, unauthorized mass posting, unverified TikTok Shop API assumptions, product fulfillment, custom video-generation engine, and paid services without explicit budget approval.

## Gates
A. Evidence: every important fact has provenance or is marked unknown.
B. Eligibility: program, account, product, and linking method checked against current official sources.
C. Economics: commission terms and costs shown transparently; unknown inputs remain unknown.
D. Content: each factual claim is supported or removed; disclosure is present.
E. Approval: a human approves the exact version to publish.
F. Measurement: content ID, channel, timestamp, metric source, and observation window recorded.
G. Repeatability: run at least three differentiated concepts before automating more. This is process validation, not statistical proof.

## MVP success
One content package is approved and published through an allowed workflow, real outcomes are recorded where accessible, and another operator can reproduce the process.
