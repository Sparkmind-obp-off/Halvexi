# TikTok-First Channel Operations

TikTok is the first channel hypothesis because short-form visual demonstrations suit physical products. A new account is not assumed to qualify for TikTok Shop affiliate features. Verify current regional, account, program, link, content, and analytics eligibility in official documentation and the actual logged-in account.

## Legitimate multi-channel portfolio
Multiple accounts are supported only for legitimate, accountable channel purposes. Each must have a clear owner, truthful identity where required, distinct audience/content purpose, compliant assets, and independent measurement. No impersonation, ban evasion, artificial engagement, fake independent endorsements, automated account warming, or flooding recommendations with duplicates.

## Channel record
Channel ID/handle, responsible owner, niche/audience, eligibility state and verification date, affiliate program, allowed linking method, content capacity, brand restrictions, metrics, and incidents.

## Starting plan
1. Begin with one account the operator controls.
2. Verify affiliate eligibility, permitted links, and disclosure requirements.
3. Run a small sustainable test and record outcomes.
4. Expand only when the first workflow is compliant and measurable and the next channel has a legitimate distinct purpose.
5. Keep human review and approval for publishing. Automation may prepare drafts/reminders; publishing stays manual unless an authorized official API is verified.

## Pre-post checks
Confirm product link, current price/availability claims, disclosure, footage/music rights, caption, claims, account status, approval version, and measurement plan. Record post URL/ID, channel, product, content version, publication time, and tracking method.

## Verification record
For each rule, retain official URL, access date, region, and exact requirement observed. If not verified, mark unverified and do not assume eligibility.
